Veil Bench

The readability veil is the one accessibility fix that was deferred, because the case for it was measured on a single image and extrapolated to six themes. This measures it properly — real asset, real palettes, every theme, every height.

This models the compositor, it is not the compositor. Canvas filters approximate SwiftUI's .saturation and .contrast, and the sampled photograph is downscaled. Treat a result as a candidate to confirm on device, not as a verdict.

What the bench found

The problem is dark-theme only

All three light themes already clear AA on every text token, un-carded, at the worst sampled cell — Ghee Lamp Ivory reaches 4.98:1 even on tertiary. The three dark themes fail: 3.21:1 secondary and 2.85:1 tertiary on Deep Sandhya.

The deferred proposal would have re-tuned all six. Only three need touching, and the veil code already branches on isLight.

The three dark themes agree

Deep Sandhya, Temple Dawn and Kumkum Night need the same correction to within one step: +0.15 on all three stops clears secondary, +0.21 clears tertiary. That convergence is the evidence the single-asset measurement could not supply.

Measured, not extrapolated: 4.53 / 4.64 / 4.51:1 on tertiary at +0.21.

There are two routes, and they differ

Raise the veil to 0.55 / 0.51 / 0.83, or leave the veil alone and lower the photograph from 0.78 to 0.52. Both reach AA. The second keeps the gradient exactly as designed and makes the imagery more subordinate, which is what the plan asks for in so many words.

The originally proposed 0.56 / 0.60 / 0.74 over-raises the middle stop and under-raises the bottom.

How to read the sweep. Each row composites the theme's own background, the photograph through that theme's saturation and contrast, the three-stop veil, and — when Surface is Carded — the glass card at its opacity. Text is onSurface at the token's opacity. The ratio shown is the worst of eleven sampled heights, because the veil is a vertical gradient and the middle stop sits where most un-carded content lands.

    
Thresholds. 4.5:1 is WCAG AA for body text; 3:1 is AA for text at 18.66px bold or 24px regular, marked large. The shipped app fails on un-carded content in the middle band — that is what this bench exists to fix, and what a single-asset measurement could not responsibly justify changing across six themes.
