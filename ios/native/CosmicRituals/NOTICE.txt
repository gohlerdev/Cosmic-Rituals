The bundled world_cities.tsv file is a population-sorted transformation of
GeoNames cities15000. It retains ASCII place name, latitude, longitude, IANA
timezone identifier, and country name. Coordinate values are rounded to four
decimal places. The catalog is bundled for offline location lookup. Cosmic
Rituals is not affiliated with or endorsed by GeoNames.
